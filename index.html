About

What is GDG on Campus?

Google Developer Groups (GDG) on Campus is a program that helps college students learn, practise and share Google technologies through peer-to-peer events and projects. Our chapter at RIT Hassan brings that community to the Computer Science and Engineering Department.

🎯

Our Mission

Make hands-on tech learning accessible to every student, regardless of branch or experience level, through free, practical, student-led sessions.

🔭

Our Vision

A campus where students graduate not only with degrees but with real projects, portfolios, mentors and a network of builders.

🤝

Our Values

Inclusivity, curiosity, collaboration and learning by doing. Everyone is welcome to ask, build, break things and try again.

🌱

Our Objectives

Run regular workshops, support project teams, prepare students for hackathons and internships, and connect them to the wider GDG network.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join GDG on Campus RIT Hassan?

Any student of RIT from any branch or year. No prior coding experience is needed, only curiosity.

Is there a membership fee?

Our regular learning sessions are community-driven. Check each event card for any specific details.

Do I need my own laptop?

A laptop helps for workshops. If you do not have one, team up with a friend, as we encourage pair learning.

How can I become a core team member?

Attend events, contribute to projects and watch for recruitment announcements. We look for consistency and attitude over experience.
